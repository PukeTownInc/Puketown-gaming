# Product Scope — Puke Town Gaming

## Surfaces

- Responsive website and installable mobile PWA.
- Discord community and bot using the shared backend.
- Xbox-first provider research, PlayStation second. Steam is not a launch priority.

## Core areas

Dashboard; console connections; gaming activity; achievements; leaderboards; weekly challenges; XP wallet; Reward Credits wallet; rewards catalogue; community; settings and privacy controls.

## Progression and rewards

Gaming XP is non-transferable, non-redeemable progression. It may unlock levels, ranks, cosmetics and XP-only challenges.

Reward Credits are a separate liability. They may be issued only from verified, cleared and provider-approved revenue, within campaign budgets and available reserves. No credit issuance or redemption is active in this foundation build.

Potential catalogue categories: gaming gift cards, Discord Nitro, cash/PayPal, gaming equipment, Steam games, crypto and exclusive memberships. Each category requires provider, legal, tax, regional and fulfilment review before launch.

## Advertising

Rewarded advertising is optional and must not gate core gameplay, account linking, achievement viewing or ordinary weekly challenges. Ad benefits must be permitted by the specific advertising provider. Never assume a provider allows points redeemable for cash, crypto or gift cards. Any ad-based Reward Credits require written provider approval and cleared revenue funding.

## Explicit non-goals for Milestone 1

- No live console account linking or provider API calls.
- No live advertising or ad SDK.
- No reward purchases, payouts, cash/crypto transfers or redemptions.
- No public leaderboard claims based on unverified activity.
- No modification of the existing Puke Town PWA.
